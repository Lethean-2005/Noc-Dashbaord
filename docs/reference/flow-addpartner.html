How a partner and its contacts actually get created

Add Partner writes one Partner row and its first Contact row together, in a single atomic call — there is no such thing as a partner with zero contacts. Every contact after that goes through Add Contact, which reuses the same partner_id and never touches the partner record itself. That loop has no cap: one partner can end up with as many contact rows as you add.

Edit updates one contact's fields plus the partner's name/type in the same call. Delete removes exactly one contact row. Nothing in the app removes a Partner row — that gap is marked below and is intentionally on hold.

Routes, at a glance

RouteTouchesNotes
POST /partner-contact/create create Partner + Contact #1 Atomic — both rows or neither. All 13 fields required.
POST /partner-contact/add-contact loop new Contact row Reuses the row's partner_id; partner name/type/domain locked read-only. No limit on repeats.
POST /partner-contact/update 1 Contact row + partner name/type Matched by partner_id + contact_id, not by name text.
POST /partner-contact/delete 1 Contact row Partner row and its other contacts are untouched.
— gap Partner row No route removes a Partner. Declined for now — see below.
Delete Partner (a real cascading delete of the partner + all its contacts) was discussed and explicitly held off on for now — not built.
