Notifications & Alerting

Background alert jobs watch the same local cache every page reads from — nothing here puts extra load on MSSQL or PostgreSQL. Bot tokens and chat/group IDs live in the app's environment file; the destinations below are illustrative labels for the role each one plays.

Telegram Alerts

Four independent bots run on their own schedules, each posting to its own chat as a table image with a styled .xlsx attachment, falling back to plain text if image rendering isn't available.

Alert Watches Illustrative Destination Default Schedule
Site Down Incident Site Down, past a configurable outage threshold NOC – Site Down Alerts Every 4 hours
INC-WO Site Down Open INC-WO Site Down rows, L2 On-Site only NOC – INC-WO Site Down Every 15 minutes
INC-WO Category Power Open INC-WO Power rows, L2 On-Site only NOC – INC-WO Power Every 15 minutes
INC-WO General Every other open INC-WO category, L2 On-Site only NOC – INC-WO General Every 15 minutes

Teams Webhook Alerts

A separate scheduler posts the same class of active-alarm summaries to a Microsoft Teams channel via incoming webhook, on a single shared interval. Each card's webhook URL is configured independently, so an individual card can be silenced without affecting the others.
