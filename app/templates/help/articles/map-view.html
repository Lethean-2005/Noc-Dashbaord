Map View

  • Use the status filter (top left) to switch between On Air, Planned, Other, and Site Down, or a specific vendor under On Air.
  • Search by site code, POP code, or village name using the search box.
  • Draw a boundary by searching a province, district, or commune to see a scoped list and export.
  • Clicking any site or POP dot opens a popup with its name, status, and — for a site with an open Site Down incident — a direct link to that incident.
  • The map refreshes itself automatically — no manual reload needed for status or Site Down changes on sites already shown.
