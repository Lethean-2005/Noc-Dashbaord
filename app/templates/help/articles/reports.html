Reports & Exporting

The Custom Dashboard (Reports) works exactly like the Overall Dashboard — live badge counts with their own drilldowns — combining eight cards onto one page: Current Site Down, Alarm Site Down, Alarm Completely Site Down, Alarm Main Failure, INC-WO Site Down, Alarm DG On, INC-WO Resolved, and IT NODE.

Every drilldown table, the Map, and Reports each have their own export control — CSV for tabular drilldowns, styled .xlsx for the Map and Reports.
